# Timeline navigation

Click the time ruler to place the playhead at that source time. Click the waveform to place the playhead and select the clip or cut underneath. Both gestures support dragging the playhead. Trim handles keep their normal editing behavior; no Alt modifier is needed. Press Space or Play to start playback; seeking during playback keeps it running. Edited playback continues to skip cut ranges.

Hold Shift and scroll to pan left or right anywhere over the timeline, including its toolbar. Scrolling down moves right; scrolling up moves left. Zoom in first if the complete recording already fits in the window. Unmodified scrolling over the track still zooms around the pointer.
