# Timeline navigation

Click the time ruler to place the playhead at that source time. Alt-click the waveform to place it without changing the selected clip or activating trim handles. Both gestures support dragging the playhead. Press Space or Play to start playback; seeking during playback keeps it running. Edited playback continues to skip cut ranges.

Hold Shift and scroll to pan left or right anywhere over the timeline, including its toolbar. Scrolling down moves right; scrolling up moves left. Zoom in first if the complete recording already fits in the window. Unmodified scrolling over the track still zooms around the pointer.
